PinkLedger

Boutique POS & Ledger

Download for Android

Version 1.0 · ~8 MB · Android 7.0+

What it does

📦

Inventory & batches

Track every garment by name, category, quality class, and supplier batch.

💳

Sales & receipts

Record a sale in seconds. Send a receipt on WhatsApp. Handle layaways and customer balances.

📊

Profit & insights

See today's revenue, net profit, slow movers, and which class actually makes you money.

🧾

Expenses & Z-Report

Log store costs, personal drawings, and outside capital. Close the day with a full cash reconciliation.

📶

Works offline

Every sale, expense, and update saves to your phone instantly. Syncs to the cloud when online.

Requirements

• Android 7.0 or newer

• ~8 MB storage

• No internet needed to use — only for cloud backup
